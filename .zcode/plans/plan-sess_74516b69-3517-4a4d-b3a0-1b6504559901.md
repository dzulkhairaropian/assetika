# Implementation Plan: 100% Form Parity & Icon/Color Mapping Synchronization for Asset Statuses & Ticket Statuses

## Overview
Menyelaraskan **100% identik** antara form Asset Statuses (`pages/master/statuses/create.blade.php` & `edit.blade.php`) dan Ticket Statuses / Admin Lookups (`admin/lookups/form.blade.php`). Seluruh daftar ikon pilihan (Lucide) dan pilihan warna (*color swatches*) disamakan persis di kedua form.

---

## Rincian Perubahan

### 1. Standardization of Icon & Color Options
Buat daftar ikon Lucide & warna tema yang **seragam & identik**:
- **Daftar Ikon Lucide**:
  `circle-check`, `circle-x`, `circle-alert`, `triangle-alert`, `user`, `user-check`, `users`, `bookmark`, `refresh-cw`, `rotate-ccw`, `loader`, `clock`, `check`, `x`, `thumbs-up`, `wrench`, `settings`, `monitor`, `file-text`, `archive`, `package`, `trash-2`, `help-circle`, `info`, `log-in`, `log-out`, `eye`, `download`, `search`, `database`, `key`, `cpu`, `wifi`, `dollar-sign`, `bell`, `plus`, `edit-3`, `activity`, `shield`, `star`, `lock`, `zap`.
- **Daftar Warna Tema & Swatch**:
  `primary` (#465FFF), `secondary` (#667085), `success` (#12B76A), `danger` (#F04438), `warning` (#F79009), `info` (#0BA5EC), `light` (#E4E7EC), `dark` (#1D2939), `orange` (#FB6514), `emerald` (#10B981), `purple` (#8B5CF6).

### 2. Form Layout Synchronization (`admin/lookups/form.blade.php`, `create.blade.php`, `edit.blade.php`)
- **Struktur Grid 2-Kolom**: `grid gap-6 xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1.75fr)_380px]`.
- **Input Color**:
  - Kolom input warna bertipe teks yang mendukung nama tema (seperti `success`, `danger`, `primary`) maupun kode Hex (`#12B76A`).
  - Swatch warna dapat diklik untuk mengisi input secara instan.
- **Input Icon**:
  - Pill button ikon Lucide yang dapat diklik langsung memasukkan nama ikon ke input.
- **Card Containers & Subtitles**:
  - Paritas subtitle description pada breadcrumb dan layout tombol simpan / cancel di sidebar kanan.
